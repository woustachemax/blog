I deleted every post here, I’m great at second guessing my abilities, which led me to use AI extensively to fix grammar, but as is the case with AI, it started generating sentences and my blog now sounds like your most annoying, preachy yet clueless connection on LinkedIn.

So, I’m starting fresh, building Hindsight, a thing that watches traders on Hyperliquid (a crypto exchange where all trades are public) and tells you if they’re any good or just got lucky. The maths runs in Rust, which I’m learning as I go, and later there’s a Python agent on top that keeps an eye on the big wallets for me. And Imma be a 100% myself this time, even if it comes with a shit ton of typos, a few grammatical errors and absent punctuations.

tl;dr I let AI write my blog, it got weird, I wiped it, and now I’m building Hindsight and writing about it myself.
